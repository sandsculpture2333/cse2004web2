Scene Trap

Assemble your scenario. Step into the scene.

What is Scene Trap?

Scene Trap is a two-player improv dialogue game. You and another person play two characters trapped in a dramatic situation. The AI sets the scene — you act it out by taking turns writing what your character says.

There are no right answers. The goal is to stay in character, follow the rules, and push the scene forward.

Before you start

Each player opens Scene Trap on their own device. One person creates a room and shares the 4-character code. The other joins with that code. Once both players are in, the host generates the scene.

Step 1 — Build your scene

Use the three dropdowns to pick a World (the setting), a Relationship (who these two characters are to each other), and a Scene type (what kind of situation they're in). Then click Enter the Scene.

The AI will generate:

  • Scene setup — the backstory and why these two must face each other right now
  • Objective — a specific thing that must happen before the scene can end (e.g. "one of them must apologise")
  • Speaking Rule — a rule about how you must speak (e.g. "every line must start with 'I never'")

Read all three carefully before you begin.

Step 2 — Play the scene

The game enforces whose turn it is. When it is your turn, the input unlocks — type what your character says and click Speak (or press Enter). Then the other player does the same on their device.

A few rules:

  • Stay in character — speak as the character, not as yourself
  • Follow the speaking rule at all times
  • You can say anything your character would plausibly say — argue, deflect, confess, accuse
  • There is no fixed number of turns. Keep going until the objective has been reached
Step 3 — Use the tools
  • Trigger Chaos — inject a surprise event that shifts the balance. Use it when the scene feels stuck or too comfortable. Both players must incorporate it.
  • Check Ending — ask the AI to judge whether the objective has been achieved. End Manually — end the scene immediately without AI judgment.
  • Retract Last Line — remove the most recent dialogue line if it was spoken out of turn or broke the speaking rule.
  • Use Voice — speak your line into the microphone instead of typing. The transcript will fill in automatically.
How does it end?

Either player can click End Manually to end the scene immediately. The scene ends for both players and all controls are locked. Use Check Ending to ask the AI whether the objective has genuinely been achieved through dialogue.

Tips for first-timers
  • Read the scene setup out loud together before you begin — it helps both players get into character
  • The constraint is a challenge, not a suggestion. Breaking it on purpose is cheating
  • Don't rush to end the scene. The best moments happen when you stay in the tension longer
  • Chaos events are meant to be disruptive — don't ignore them, react to them

Quick Walkthrough

  1. Create or join a room Enter your name, then create a room or join with a code.
  2. Build the scene The host chooses presets or custom details and generates the scene for both players.
  3. Read the scene file Use the setup, objective, and speaking rule to understand what the scene is asking for.
  4. Take turns speaking Only the current player can add the next line.
  5. Trigger chaos After enough dialogue, chaos can introduce new evidence or shift the balance.
  6. End the scene Use Check Ending when you think the completion trigger has been met. End Manually is the fallback.
